# Homepage showroom extension

Mode: extension of the existing Experiences section. Preserve the dark navy/white/electric-blue visual vocabulary, Archivo Black and Manrope, authentic LJ imagery, four existing sports and product inquiry links, the `#experiences` anchor, the homepage hero slider and the rest of the site's navigation and routes.

The selected product becomes a dimensional perspective card with a downward image transition. The existing product list continues to drive the same selection. Previous/next controls and horizontal touch swipes offer additional ways to browse; pointer tilt is a nonessential enhancement and is disabled for reduced-motion or non-fine input. Movement should use transforms and opacity and remain usable without 3D effects.
