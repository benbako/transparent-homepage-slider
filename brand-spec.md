# LJ Sports Tech design and brand assets

- Artifact and audience: cinematic B2B landing page for indoor sports venues, entertainment operators and distributors.
- Direction: original kinetic sports-editorial design, with high-contrast black, white, electric blue and the existing orange/red company mark. Visual variance 7/10, motion 7/10, density 4/10, asset dependence 8/10, brand fidelity 6/10.
- Identity: the user-approved refined LJ Sports Tech logo is used in the shared site header and footer. The original official logo is retained at `client/public/assets/lj-logo.png` for reference; it was sourced from https://www.ljsportstech.com/.
- Product visuals: official company website assets in `client/public/assets` are used only for the relevant product categories. Original generated conceptual venue imagery is used for environmental atmosphere and is not represented as an actual product installation.
- Typography: Archivo Black / Archivo and Manrope, with bold condensed editorial headlines and comfortably sized body copy.
- Composition: cinematic full-bleed hero with asymmetric type and an interactive 3D orbit, a product-experience selector, a wide venue story, and direct contact.
- Interaction: pointer/touch-reactive low-cost WebGL accents; adaptive navigation; category controls, accessible links, subtle transitions, and a reduced-motion fallback.
- Source facts: the company designs and sells digital sports simulators and provides venue planning and installation support; see https://www.ljsportstech.com/about-us.html. Contact details: https://www.ljsportstech.com/contactus.html.
- This design references the general ambition of premium immersive entertainment sites only; it does not copy movie-power.com text, graphics, layout, or proprietary assets.
- Company imagery: `client/public/assets/company.webp` is an optimized copy of the official About page image at https://iqrorwxhnpjllq5p-static.micyjz.com/cloud/lmBpiKpqlpSRililikliko/about.jpg.

## About page design extension

The About page retains the official company-building image and existing shared LJ branding. Its interactive court-and-screen scene is an original, clearly labeled concept illustration—not a photograph of a product or installation. The company history, product development, customization and installation support come from https://www.ljsportstech.com/about-us.html; 3D venue-rendering support is described on https://www.ljsportstech.com/. The site's customer-story article describes the mission as “Make Sports Easier.” The three design values are editorial themes rather than a claim of a formally published corporate-values list.

- About galleries: the Factory photographs and certificate/patent images are sourced from LJ Sports Tech’s original About page. All original gallery positions are preserved, including repeated documents shown by the source. Document links retain the original full image, while lightweight uncropped thumbnails improve browsing; no certificate validity is independently asserted.

- About image refinement: the three user-supplied sports-experience visuals are displayed full-frame in the About hero (`client/public/assets/about-user/`), with the original company-building image retained in the story. The 25 factory images and 29 certificate/patent entries remain in their existing order and link to full-size originals; factory imagery is shown without cropping so the shape of each space remains visible.
